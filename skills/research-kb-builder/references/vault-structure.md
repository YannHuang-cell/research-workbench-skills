# Vault Structure

Prefer a small object-based structure.

## Suggested Folders

- `00 Inbox`
- `01 Projects`
- `02 Papers`
- `03 Concepts`
- `04 Methods`
- `05 Code`
- `06 Ideas`
- `07 Weekly`
- `99 Templates`

## Why This Works

- It matches how research work actually flows.
- It reduces the pressure to decide between many overlapping topic folders.
- It makes project pages the main coordination surface.

## Useful Properties

- `type`
- `status`
- `created`
- `updated`
- `projects`
- `papers`
- `methods`
- `repos`
- `ideas`
